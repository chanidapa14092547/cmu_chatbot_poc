# Bachelor of Science in Digital Innovation (International Program)
## Program Overview
The B.S. in Digital Innovation is a 4-year curriculum combining Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Business Administration, Economics, and Law.
It also offers a 2+2 Double Degree Program with Curtin University (Australia), University of Strathclyde (UK), and East China University of Science and Technology (China).

## Study Plan
Total credits and specific courses are assigned per semester.
### Year 1
- First Semester (21 credits): 001101, 204101, 206171 or 206113, 208150, 888111, GE (Elective), General Education Elective.
- Second Semester (21 credits): 001102, 204217, 206255 or 206172, 888152 or 208250, 888121, 888151, 140104.
### Year 2
- First Semester (21 credits): 001201, 204251, 206281 or 208251 or 206255, 204100 or 261111 or 953111, General Education (Electives).
- Second Semester (18 credits): 001225, 888211, 888231, 888232, 888311, Major Elective, General Education (Electives).
### Year 3-4
- Focus on Major Electives, Free Electives, and Technology Startup Projects (Pre-Project and Final Project).
