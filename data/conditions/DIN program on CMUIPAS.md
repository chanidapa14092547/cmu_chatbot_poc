# DIN program on CMUIPAS

## Bachelor of Science in Digital Innovation (International Program)

### Total Credits Required for Graduation: 132 Credits

### Curriculum Structure:

## General Education
*   General Education (Elective) / General Education Elective (Innovative Co-creator): 3 credits
*   General Education (Electives): 6 credits
*   **Total General Education: 9 credits**

## Major
*   **Major Compulsory Courses:** 78 credits
    *   This includes core subjects in English, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Digital Economy, Digital Innovation, and Technology Startup Projects.
*   **Major Elective Courses:** 42 credits
    *   Students choose 42 credits from a list of approved major elective courses.

## Free Electives
*   **Free Elective Courses:** 3 credits

### Tracks/Plans

#### 2+2 Double Degree Program
Students spend the first 2 years studying at ICDI (Chiang Mai University) and the remaining 2 years at a chosen partner university. Upon successful completion, students are awarded two degrees. Partner universities include:
*   Curtin University, Australia (Data Science program)
*   University of Strathclyde, UK (Entrepreneur program)
*   East China University of Science and Technology, China (International Economic and Trade program)

### Study Duration

Students have the option to complete their studies within a duration of either 3.5 years or 4 years.
