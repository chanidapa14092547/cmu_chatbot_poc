# MMIT-Plan-65-inter

# Curriculum Structure and Graduation Credit Requirements

The total credits required for the entire curriculum is not less than 126 credits.

## General Education (หมวดวิชาศึกษาทั่วไป)
A minimum of 30 credits

### Required Courses (วิชาบังคับ)
A minimum of 21 credits, consisting of:
*   **Learner Person (กลุ่มวิชาด้านการพัฒนาทักษะการเป็นผู้เรียนรู้):** 15 credits
    *   001101 Fundamental English 1: 3 credits
    *   001102 Fundamental English 2: 3 credits
    *   001201 Critical Reading and Effective Writing: 3 credits
    *   001229 English for Media Arts: 3 credits
    *   Select 1 course from the following:
        *   204100 Information Technology and Modern Life: 3 credits
        *   953111 Software for Everyday Life: 3 credits
*   **Innovative Co-creator (กลุ่มวิชาด้านการพัฒนาทักษะการเป็นผู้ร่วมสร้างสรรค์นวัตกรรม):** 3 credits
    *   Select 1 course from the followings:
        *   011151 Reasoning: 3 credits
        *   013110 Psychology and Daily Life: 3 credits
        *   159100 Modern World in Everyday Life: 3 credits
        *   571111 Critical Thinking and Creative Thinking: 3 credits
*   **Active Citizen (กลุ่มวิชาด้านการพัฒนาทักษะการเป็นพลเมืองที่เข้มแข็ง):** 3 credits
    *   Select 1 course from the followings:
        *   140104 Citizenship: 3 credits
        *   703103 Introduction to Entrepreneurship and Business: 3 credits

### Elective Courses (วิชาเลือก)
A minimum of 9 credits
*   A student also chooses 9 additional credits from the Learner Person and Active Citizen groups of GE elective courses.

## Major (หมวดวิชาเฉพาะ)
A minimum of 90 credits
The Major courses are divided into two plans: Work Integrated Learning Plan and Independent Study Plan.

### Work Integrated Learning Plan (แผนการเรียนรู้ร่วมกับการท างาน)

#### Core Courses (วิชาแกน)
A minimum of 33 credits

#### Major Courses (วิชาเอก)
A minimum of 57 credits

##### Major Compulsory (เอกบังคับ)
39 credits

##### Major Electives (เอกเลือก)
A minimum of 18 credits. Students choose at least 18 credits from the Major Electives.

#### Minor (วิชาโท (ถ้ามี))
A minimum of 15 credits (optional). If students choose to have a minor degree, they must register courses not less than 15 credits with academic advisor's approval, increasing the total curriculum credits by at least 15 credits.

### Independent Study Plan (แผนการค้นคว้าอิสระ)

#### Core Courses (วิชาแกน)
A minimum of 33 credits

#### Major Courses (วิชาเอก)
A minimum of 57 credits

##### Major Compulsory (เอกบังคับ)
33 credits

##### Major Electives (เอกเลือก)
A minimum of 24 credits. Students choose at least 24 credits, but must select at least 9 credits from advanced courses (level 400) from the Major Electives.

#### Minor (วิชาโท (ถ้ามี))
A minimum of 15 credits (optional). If students choose to have a minor degree, they must register courses not less than 15 credits with academic advisor's approval, increasing the total curriculum credits by at least 15 credits.

## Free Electives (หมวดวิชาเลือกเสรี)
A minimum of 6 credits
