**Total Credits for Graduation:** Not less than 123 credits

## General Education
**Total:** 30 credits
*   **Language and Communication (กลุ่มวิชาภาษาและการสื่อสาร):** 12 credits
*   **Humanities and Social Sciences (กลุ่มวิชามนุษยศาสตร์และสังคมศาสตร์):** 6 credits (Select 2 courses)
*   **Science and Mathematics (กลุ่มวิชาวิทยาศาสตร์และคณิตศาสตร์):** 9 credits (Select 3 courses)
*   **Learning through Activities (กลุ่มวิชาการเรียนรู้ผ่านกิจกรรม):** 3 credits
    *   Compulsory: 955100 (1 credit) and 955101 (1 credit)
    *   Select 1 additional 1-credit course from the provided list.

## Minor (วิชาโท) (If any)
**Total:** Not less than 15 credits
*   Students wishing to take a minor can choose any minor offered by Chiang Mai University, with the advisor's approval. These credits will be added to the total program credits, increasing the total by at least 15 credits.

## Free Electives (หมวดวิชาเลือกเสรี)
**Total:** Not less than 6 credits (Select courses from outside the College of Arts, Media, and Technology)

## Language of Instruction
*   Specific courses (within the College of Arts, Media, and Technology) are taught in English.
*   General Education courses and Free Elective courses (other courses outside the College of Arts, Media, and Technology) are taught in Thai.
