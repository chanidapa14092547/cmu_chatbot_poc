2.3 Minor : a minimum of 15
1. Students, who wish to enroll in minor courses, can select minor courses in any field according to Chiang Mai University
Regulation on Minor Courses for Chiangmai University students with the minimum of 15 credits, with the approval of the
advisor. This will result in addition of minimum of 15 credits to the total credits.
2. A student who does not desire to register for minor courses must register for the extra of at least 15 credits for major
elective courses aside from your current plan as shown in the following plans. The total credits of major elective courses
must be at least 63 credits.
Plan I : Finance and Banking
For those extra of at least 15 credits for major elective courses, a student with Field of Concentration 1: Finance and
Banking can register for the major elective courses chosen from Management and Entrepreneurship field of
concentration (703…) and/or from Marketing field of concentration (705…) as listed below
Plan II : Management and Entrepreneurship
Field of Concentration 2: Management and Entrepreneurship can register for the major elective courses chosen from
Finance and Banking field of concentration (702…) and/or from Marketing field of concentration (705…) as listed below
Plan III : Marketing
Field of Study 3: Marketing can register for the major elective courses chosen from Finance and Banking field of
concentration (702…) and/or from Management and Entrepreneurship field of concentration (703…) as listed below
