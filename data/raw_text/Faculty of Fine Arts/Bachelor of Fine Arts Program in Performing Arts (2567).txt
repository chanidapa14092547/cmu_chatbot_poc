According to the Chiang Mai University regulation on the Minor curriculum for the Chiang Mai University student, if
students choose to have a minor degree in a curriculum at the Chiang Mai University, they can register those courses
not less than 15 credits with the academic advisor’s approval. Hence, the total credits for the whole curriculum is
increased at least 15 credits.
