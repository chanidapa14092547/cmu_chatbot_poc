(1) Regular Plan : a minimum of 59
Among the credits earned from the major courses, a minimum of 36 credits must be from the advanced level courses
(300-400), of which at least 18 credits must be from the 400 Level courses.

(300 and higher), of which at least 18 credits must be from the 400 level courses, and 12 credits must be from the 700
level courses.

2.3.1 For student who wishes to have minor
(1) Regular Plan and Co-operative Education Plan :
